Who we are

A place where knowledge becomes a way of life.

Urwatul Wuthqaa (URWA) is an Islamic learning and community initiative based in Kenya, welcoming learners near and far. We create opportunities to study the Qur’an, strengthen understanding of the deen and build meaningful bonds rooted in faith.

Our aim is to make learning accessible, purposeful and grounded in sincerity, consistency and good character.

IkhlaasSincerity in seeking and sharing knowledge.
ItqaanCare, quality and excellence in learning.
RifqGentleness, patience and compassion.
UkhuwwahCommunity, support and sisterhood.
